## What Makes a Commercial Pool Different?

Commercial pools in Dubai are regulated by Dubai Municipality and must comply with stricter standards than residential pools:

- **Minimum depth requirements** depending on pool type (lap, diving, leisure)
- **Filtration capacity** — commercial pools must turn over the full water volume every 4–6 hours
- **Disinfection systems** — UV + chlorine or salt chlorination with automated dosing
- **Safety features** — anti-entrapment drains, pool lifts (where applicable), depth markers
- **Accessibility compliance** — ramps or steps meeting DM standards
- **DM NOC (No Objection Certificate)** — required before construction begins

Alwahaa Technical Services manages the full DM permit and NOC process for commercial clients.

## Types of Commercial Pools We Build in Dubai

### Hotel & Resort Pools

The most visible commercial pool type in Dubai. We build:

- **Leisure pools** — freeform shapes with beach entries, tanning ledges, waterfall features
- **Lap pools** — 25m and 50m competition or fitness lanes
- **Infinity pools** — vanishing-edge pools facing skyline, sea, or landscape
- **Rooftop pools** — engineered for elevated hotel terraces with full structural load analysis
- **Pool complexes** — multiple connected bodies with separate heating and filtration zones

Dubai hotels demand the highest finishes — premium Italian glass mosaic tile, stainless steel overflow channels, LED lighting systems, and automated water management are standard for 4- and 5-star properties.

### Residential Compound & Community Pools

Community development pools serve hundreds of residents and require:

- High-capacity filtration (commercial-grade pumps and filter banks)
- Multiple inlet/outlet configurations for even circulation
- Automated chemical dosing systems
- Separate wading/children's pool zones
- Shade structures, lifeguard stations, and safety equipment integration

We have built community pools for compounds in Jumeirah, Al Barsha, Mirdif, Damac Hills, and Dubai Hills.

### Sports & Fitness Club Pools

Lap pools and competition pools for gyms, sports clubs, and school facilities across Dubai:

- 25m lanes (minimum 6 lanes for competitive use)
- Lane rope anchor points and timing systems integration
- High-flow filtration to handle peak load (300+ bathers/day)
- Overflow gutter systems with balance tanks

### Water Parks & Leisure Attractions

Beyond standard pools, we design and build:

- Wave pool foundations and hydraulic infrastructure
- Lazy river channels
- Splash pad and water play structures
- Filtration and chemical systems for high-throughput attractions

## Commercial Pool Construction Process in Dubai

### 1. Site Assessment & Feasibility

We visit the site, assess soil conditions, structural constraints, existing utilities, and plot access. For rooftop pools, we coordinate with the structural engineer to confirm load capacity.

### 2. Design & Engineering

CAD drawings, hydraulic calculations, and 3D renders are prepared. The design must satisfy both the client's brief and DM technical requirements.

### 3. DM Permit Application

We submit drawings to Dubai Municipality and manage the NOC process. Timeline: typically 3–8 weeks depending on project complexity and DM queue.

### 4. Construction

Commercial pool construction follows this sequence:

| Phase | Activity |
|-------|----------|
| Excavation | Machine digging to approved dimensions |
| Rebar | Structural steel cage fabrication and installation |
| Shotcrete | Pneumatically applied concrete shell |
| Waterproofing | Multi-layer membrane — crystalline + sheet |
| Plumbing | Returns, inlets, main drains, equipment vault |
| Tiling | Interior finish application |
| Equipment | Pumps, filters, heaters, UV systems, BMS integration |
| Commissioning | Water fill, chemical startup, load testing |
| Handover | Staff training, documentation, warranty |

### 5. Handover & Warranty

We provide full documentation — as-built drawings, equipment manuals, maintenance schedules — and a 12-month construction warranty on structural works.

## What to Look for in a Commercial Pool Contractor in Dubai

**1. Proven commercial track record** — Ask for hotel and resort project references. Residential contractors do not always have the experience to manage commercial specifications.

**2. DM permit capability** — The contractor must be licensed to submit DM NOC applications. Unlicensed operators cannot legally build commercial pools in Dubai.

**3. In-house engineering** — Commercial pools need hydraulic calculations, structural coordination, and equipment specification. Contractors who outsource these typically cause delays.

**4. Equipment brand partnerships** — Commercial pools require Hayward, Pentair, Fluidra, or equivalent commercial-grade equipment. Avoid contractors specifying residential equipment in commercial installations.

**5. After-handover support** — Commercial pools need regular maintenance. A contractor who also provides AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract) services reduces coordination overhead.
